Topple House

Line up dominoes through every room of the house, up onto the furniture and over the gaps with catapults, then knock it all down. Each room has a job to do. The whole house is yours to play with.

BuildDrag one finger to lay a line, on the floor or on any tabletop outlined in gold. Start on an existing domino to branch off it. A line that runs off a table edge drops onto whatever is below.
GadgetsCatapults and trebuchets throw their marble when anything bumps them, from any side. Drag the gold target to where the marble should land, on the floor or up on the furniture; drop it on a goal and it locks on and turns green. The trebuchet throws twice as far. A ramp lets marbles roll down from a ledge, a pulley lift raises its marble to a set height, and a trampoline throws any marble that lands on it.
Look aroundTwo fingers pinch to zoom, drag to pan and twist to turn the house. One finger on the empty table orbits. The layers button switches overhead, angled and low views.
MoveTap a domino to select it, hold to grab its whole line, drag the gold handle to turn it. On a computer, right-drag orbits and scroll zooms.
Preview, then runPreview plays a test and stands everything back up. Run is the attempt that counts.
Start over: clears every room's dominoes, gadgets, stars and best scores. Settings stay.

Workbench

Stretch down the stairs Lines drawn down a staircase space out automatically, since each domino falls onto a lower step.
Shaky hands Like real life, setting a domino down next to a standing one can knock the whole line over. Builders leave a gap every so often and close it last.
Slow-motion playback Plays previews and runs at quarter speed.
